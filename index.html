Simple video slot game

An example of a simple 5x4 video slot game with 8 winning lines.
Features:
  • Winning lines are counted from right to left. A line of minimum 3 winning symbols pays out.
  • "Wild" is a wild symbol that substitutes any other symbol on a winning line.
  • "Scatter1" is a scatter symbol that pays out 10x, 20x, or 30x the bet if 3 or more symbols appear on any positions. Only one "Scatter1" can appear on any reel.
  • "Scatter2" is a stacked scatter symbol that can appear on the 3 middle reels. If all 3 middle reels are covered with "Scatter2" symbols, the game pays 100x the bet.

Video slot with free spins

An example of a 5x3 video slot game with free spins.
Features:
  • A minimum of 2 winning symbols on a winning line or scattered across all reels pays out.
  • During free spins, symbols on a winning line are counted not only from left to right but can also be scattered across the winning line definition.
  • During free spins, symbol sequences are different from the base game's ones. Sequences during free spins do not contain scatter symbols, so free spins cannot be re-triggered.
  • During free spins, all wins are multiplied by x2.
  • This example also demonstrates the usage of Simulation to obtain the desired game outcomes.

Video slot with sticky re-spin

An example of a 5x3 video slot game with sticky re-spin feature. Every winning combination triggers the re-spin during which all the winning symbols are held on their places. The re-spins continue as long as there are new wins.

Cascading cluster pays

An example of a cascading (tumbling) 6x5 cluster-pay video slot. Wins are groups of 5+ adjacent same-symbol cells rather than paylines; winning clusters are removed, the grid collapses and refills, and evaluation repeats until nothing wins. Each cascade step pays with an escalating multiplier (x1, x2, x3, ...).

Megaways-style ways-to-win

An example of a Megaways-style video slot. Each of the 6 reels draws its own row count at random every round (2 to 6 rows), so the grid shape changes every spin. Wins are "ways to win" - for a symbol, the product of how many matching cells sit in each consecutive reel from the left - rather than fixed paylines.

Growing grid bonus

An example of a growing-grid feature. The 5x3 grid grows by one row on every winning round (up to 5x6), giving the "Gem" scatter symbol more room to land and bigger wins more room to happen - a losing round resets the grid back down to its starting size.

Value pay with multiplier wilds

An example combining two independent win styles: "Coin" symbols pay their own value for every occurrence anywhere on the grid (value pay), while "WildX2"/"WildX3"/"WildX5" are ordinary line wilds that also multiply whatever line win they end up part of - scoped to line wins only, so the coins are unaffected.

Verifiable spin

An example of a reproducible round using SeededRandomNumberGenerator instead of the default Math.random()-based RNG. A "Verify last spin" button replays the session's seed from scratch and confirms it reproduces the exact same stop positions - useful for dispute resolution or certification test suites, not a substitute for a secure RNG in real-money play.
